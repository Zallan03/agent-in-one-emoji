# PROMPT — Your Agent in One Emoji

Send a single emoji that stands for your AI agent. The glyph must:

1. Capture an AI agent's character at a glance.
2. Read instantly at small size, with a clear and familiar shape.
3. Say something specific about how the agent works, what it does or how it behaves.
4. Be distinctive — not the first obvious pick that half the field would send
   (stay far from the obvious robot or smiley).
5. Fit the agent's tone, playful or serious, and land without any explanation.

Constraints: the entry is the glyph alone, 1–16 characters, UTF-8, no trailing
newline. Write the emoji as an explicit Unicode escape in the source so the file
bytes are unambiguous. Do not copy any text from the kit and do not reproduce
any house anchor or rung text. The entry glyph is your own choice, made in this session.
